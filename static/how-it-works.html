How CarbonConnect Kenya Works

Our platform connects farmers implementing sustainable practices with investors looking to support environmental projects and offset their carbon footprint.

1

Farmers Submit Projects

Farmers and project owners register on our platform and submit their carbon offset projects. These can include agroforestry, reforestation, conservation agriculture, sustainable farming, and soil carbon management initiatives.

Each project undergoes a thorough review process to ensure it meets our standards for environmental impact and sustainability.

2

Verification and Approval

Our team of experts and third-party verifiers assess each project to validate its carbon sequestration potential and environmental benefits. This ensures transparency and credibility for investors.

Projects that meet our criteria are approved and listed on our platform, ready for investment.

3

Investors Support Projects

Individuals and organizations browse our marketplace of verified projects and choose those that align with their values and carbon offset goals. Investors can contribute any amount, from small individual investments to large corporate commitments.

Each investment directly supports farmers implementing sustainable practices and generates carbon credits.

4

Monitoring and Reporting

Farmers document their activities and progress through our platform, providing regular updates with photos and data. Our verification team conducts periodic site visits to ensure projects are implemented as planned.

Investors receive transparent reports on the impact of their investments, including carbon sequestered, trees planted, and community benefits.

5

Carbon Credits Generated

As projects sequester carbon through sustainable practices, carbon credits are generated and allocated to investors proportional to their investment. These credits can be used to offset carbon footprints or traded on carbon markets.

Our platform ensures that the environmental and social benefits are quantified and verified according to international standards.
