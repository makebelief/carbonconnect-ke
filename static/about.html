About CarbonConnect Kenya

We're building a more sustainable future by connecting smallholder farmers with carbon markets.

Our Story

CarbonConnect Kenya launches in 2025 with a simple but powerful vision: to democratize access to carbon markets for smallholder farmers in Kenya while accelerating climate action. We recognize that while Kenya's agricultural sector has enormous potential for carbon sequestration, most smallholder farmers lack the resources, knowledge, and connections to participate in carbon markets.

Our platform aims to bridge this gap by leveraging technology, community engagement, and transparent verification processes. We're building a marketplace that will benefit both farmers implementing sustainable practices and individuals or organizations looking to offset their carbon footprint.

Our Mission

To empower smallholder farmers in Kenya to implement sustainable agricultural practices, access carbon markets, and improve their livelihoods while contributing to climate change mitigation.

Our Vision

A future where sustainable agriculture is the norm, smallholder farmers are financially rewarded for their environmental stewardship, and communities thrive in harmony with nature.

Our Values

  • Transparency: We believe in complete transparency in all our operations, from project verification to carbon credit allocation.
  • Community-Centered: We prioritize the needs and voices of the communities we work with, ensuring they benefit directly from carbon projects.
  • Environmental Integrity: We maintain rigorous standards to ensure real, measurable, and permanent carbon sequestration.
  • Innovation: We continuously explore new technologies and approaches to make carbon markets more accessible and effective.
  • Collaboration: We aim to work closely with farmers, communities, NGOs, government agencies, and private sector partners to achieve our mission.
