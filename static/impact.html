Environmental Impact

Our platform aims to facilitate significant environmental benefits across Kenya. Through planned agroforestry, reforestation, and sustainable agriculture projects, we project to help sequester carbon, restore degraded lands, protect biodiversity, and improve soil health.

Vision for Success

Here are some examples of the transformative impact we aim to achieve through our platform:

Transforming Degraded Land

Sample Project, Nakuru County

Our vision includes helping farmers transform 5-acre degraded lands into thriving agroforestry systems with over 2,000 trees and diverse crops, targeting 300% income increase while sequestering carbon.

Community Forest Restoration Goals

Planned Initiative, Kakamega

We aim to support community-led initiatives to restore 200 hectares of degraded forest land, creating sustainable livelihoods for 150 families while protecting vital water sources.

Youth Climate Action Initiative

Proposed Project, Nairobi

We plan to support young people from urban areas in creating urban gardens and tree nurseries, generating income from carbon credits while improving their environment.
